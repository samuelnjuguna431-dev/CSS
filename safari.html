About the Safari Rally

The Safari Rally is one of the toughest motorsport events in the world, known for its rugged terrain and unpredictable weather
conditions. Set in the heart of Kenya, this rally tests the endurance of both drivers and machines.

Rally Highlights

  • Challenging off-road courses
  • Iconic Wildlife Views
  • International competitors

Why Kenya?

Kenya's landscapes, from the savannahs to the mountains, provide the perfect backdrop for this epic rally. The Safari Rally brings
together adventure, nature, and motorsport in a unique blend.
