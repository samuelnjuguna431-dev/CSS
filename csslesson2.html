Welcome to Kenya


Kenya is one of Africa's top tourist destinations, known for its beautiful wildlife, stunning landscapes, and rich
cultural heritage. Visitors can enjoy exciting safaris, relax on the white sandy beaches of the Kenyan coast, and
explore vibrant cities such as Nairobi and Mombasa.


The maasai Mara National Reserve is famous for the Great
Migration, where millions of wildbeests and zebras cross the plains
each year. Kenya is also home to Mount Kenya, Africa's second-
highest mountain, and several national parks that protect elephants,
lions, rhinos, giraffes, and many other species. Tourism plays a major
role in the country's economy and attracts visitors from around the
world.
